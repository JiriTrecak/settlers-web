# Vanguard campaign

**Campaign → Vanguard → Mission 1 — Prologue** begins the ant campaign.

The Marshal travels with one Warrior and one Archer. Follow the lantern road to the old crossing. Two wolves ambush the party when the Marshal reaches it. Defeat both wolves to secure the crossing, then continue north to reclaim Lantern Rise. If the Marshal dies, the mission fails.

The party starts selected. Use the normal RTS controls: right-click to move, or **A**, then click the ground to advance while engaging enemies. The Marshal starts with an ability point to spend. There is no base to build or economy to manage in this mission.

Portrait dialogue appears above the control panel. The current objective stays in the upper-right corner. Major dialogue pauses combat and commands; short inline comments let play continue. After the final exchange, the results screen returns you to mission selection.

The normal **Save / Load** controls preserve mission progress, including the ambush, dialogue timing and combat state. Campaign missions are tagged in their map documents and do not appear in Skirmish or multiplayer's skirmish map list.

This first mission is single-player. Network campaign lobbies, cross-mission hero progression, voice-over and branching dialogue are not implemented yet.

See [mission scripting](/development/mission-scripting) to create or edit missions.

## The expanded prologue

The 256 × 256 Hollow has a lantern road leading from the southern gate, through the old crossing, past the lake, and up to Lantern Rise. After the two-wolf ambush, find a fleeing watch patrol and defeat the ogre pursuing it. At least one watch soldier must survive. A cinematic briefing follows; survivors then join your army, retaining their health. Finish with the three-wolf encounter at Lantern Rise. The Marshal must survive. Winning the final battle completes the mission.

Three side trails are optional: the abandoned convoy awards a **Barkguard Charm**, the moss shrine a **Trailkeeper’s Flask**, and the lakeside ogre a **Thornband**. These rewards are guaranteed after clearing their camp. Pick them up with the Marshal. The ogre is the more dangerous detour.

The Marshal can reach **level 2** in this mission. The main route provides enough experience; further XP does not accumulate beyond the cap. Major conversations pause gameplay and use cinematic bars and a closer camera. Scout comments on side trails happen during normal play.

## Mission stages

Your primary objective advances through **Reach the old crossing → Secure the crossing → Find the missing watch → Save the watch → Reclaim Lantern Rise**. Completed stages remain in the objective panel’s history. Optional cache objectives appear when you discover their side trails and complete when the defending camp is cleared.

The rescued watch soldiers become normal controllable units after their briefing. Include them in your selection or control group before marching on Lantern Rise.

The prologue opens in rain: the three companions run in from the right, gather around the center of the view, and turn toward one another for the briefing. Controls return when the conversation ends. A full day/night cycle now lasts ten minutes at normal game speed.
