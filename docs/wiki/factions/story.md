# World and campaigns

::: info Narrative direction
This page records the agreed story pitch. Campaign missions, faction-specific campaign mechanics and antagonist names are still in development. Skirmish is the playable mode today.
:::

**Under the Canopy** is a modern economic builder and RTS at insect scale. The fantasy is watching a living colony's production and movement click into place, then watching that economy go to war.

## The Breaking of the Old Pact

Long ago, three lineages sealed an older force and agreed to compete without shattering its prison. Generations of rivalry weakened the pact.

The antagonist is an ancient fungal consciousness: patient, intelligent and hungry for silent order. _The Silent Network_, _Pale Root_ and _the Hollow_ are working names. It assimilates colonies into a single unthinking body. Familiar workers returning changed and vanished colonies reappearing Hollowed make the threat personal before it becomes epic.

## Three campaigns, one changing world

1. **Ants — The Deepening.** Expansion, rivalry and discovery lead the player's first empire to dig too far. Breaking the seals is the catastrophe that ends the campaign. The player should feel responsible.
2. **Beetles — the middle campaign.** The world is already damaged. Containment, sacrifice and the old pact become central. Beetles now occupy the faction slot originally proposed for Termites; the details of their story and economy remain open.
3. **Bees — the finale.** The height and reach of the canopy reveal the true scale of the crisis. Remnants of the lineages must cooperate, and the confrontation reaches the heart of the Network.

## Tone

Tragic and epic, earned gradually: hubris, consequences, the price of empire, and the possibility of unity while the forest is dying. The characters can be appealing and stylized without becoming childish. Armor, tools and buildings should feel like parts of the same world.
