### Root cause: water self-occlusion under VSM

A temporary zero-opacity water diagnostic exposed an almost entirely shadowed bed and striped shoreline. Inspection of Three.js WebGLShadowMap confirmed that VSM renders shadow receivers into its depth pass even when castShadow is false. The water receiver therefore wrote an opaque plane into the shadow map above the entire riverbed.

Added a dedicated depth material that discards water fragments in the shadow pass while retaining normal surface shadow reception. It is disposed with WaterLayer. Removed the temporary diagnostic. The final live capture now shows riverbed stones and tree shadows beneath/across the translucent surface, with the fine shoreline stripes eliminated. Evidence: `tmp/visual-audit/river-bed-shadow-diagnostic.jpg` and `tmp/visual-audit/river-vsm-bed-fixed.jpg`. Production build passed. This is a significant rendering correction, but full daylight reference fidelity remains incomplete.
