# Queensguard campaign draft

This is a new proposed twelve-mission Ant campaign for **Under the Canopy**, with a comic breakdown of 64 panels. It rebuilds the story around the Queen's political project, the General's impossible promise, and a conflict between people who have good reasons to love one another. The previous illustrated treatment remains available in [the earlier storyboard](../queensguard-comic-v2/storyboard.md).

The [agreed campaign cornerstones](../../../docs/wiki/factions/story.md) still apply: Root genuinely strengthens the Ant civilization; the Queen dies off-screen while the Guard is away; her death fractures the colony; resurrection causes an immediate lethal catastrophe; the Queen is the ancient evil's vessel; her later intervention helps a freely assembled coalition. Everything newly specified here, including names, politics, the Keeper and the Root relay, is a proposal for discussion. These are story beats to review before another drawing pass.

## The conflict that drives the campaign

Queen Ilyra wants a realm where no colony can be left to face disaster alone. She has opened her hive to displaced families, broken predatory tolls and made the Queen's Guard a force people believe will come for them. She intends to make that protection permanent by placing the neighboring Ant settlements under one military command. She genuinely protects conquered people. She also keeps their gate keys.

General Rusk believes her realm can end the kind of retreat that leaves people behind. His soldiers revere him because he comes back for them. Root makes his impossible promise seem achievable. When the Queen dies, restoring her appears to offer a rescue of both a beloved person and an entire civilization. Over time he makes the survival of that civilization depend on getting her back.

Marshal Serrik wants the neighboring settlements to defend one another without surrendering their right to decide. He and Rusk fought together before this war. His resistance has military purpose, and the Queen's expansion gives it recruits. He is capable of honoring surrender and of using that honor against a commander whose word he no longer trusts.

Their early conflict creates the later emergency. Crown garrisons, compelled oaths and centralized command work while Ilyra lives. Her death leaves the conquered settlements armed, resentful and unwilling to accept Rusk's authority. A victory at the royal hive can protect a district; it cannot make the realm believe in its crown again.

## The people the player follows

**Queen Ilyra** is warm, formidable and demanding. She knows that command sometimes means accepting a loss, a truth Rusk struggles to bear. She rewards Vey's work and orders enemy wounded treated, but reserves the military process for her own forces. Her generosity and her desire for control belong to the same person. Her later encounter with the coalition lets her witness the unity she wanted, achieved through cooperation she once doubted.

**General Rusk**, the sword hero, wants nobody who trusted him to be abandoned. His fear of failing that promise makes him increasingly unwilling to accept other people's decisions. He remains capable of love and mercy. His eventual way forward is to protect people who choose a future he cannot command.

**Warden Brann**, the shield hero, wants to build a Guard that survives the loss of any commander. He trains junior officers and recognizes honorable enemies. He initially supports Ilyra's campaign because a common defense can save lives. After her death, he is willing to surrender conquered authority to preserve the people who remain. His alternative has costs: lost territory, distrust and a difficult evacuation rather than an intact empire.

**Captain Vey**, the Root adept, is Rusk's former pupil and a practical alchemist. She wants ordinary Ant soldiers to stand against foes whose size and armor once made them helpless. She wants her discovery shared, and resents the Queen's restrictions. When the royal relay falls silent and Ilyra is found dead, she fears the connection she built may have played a part. That fear is not proof of how the Queen died. Restoration becomes her chance to save the person who trusted her and repair an uncertain harm. She launches an operation that damages Brann's negotiations, while eventually giving him the safe Root process she wanted shared. She helps make the catastrophe possible; she must also choose to interrupt it, even when doing so destroys her greatest achievement.

**Tarn**, an ordinary shield-bearer, gives the soldiers a continuing human-scale presence. Rusk rescues him, Brann teaches him to command, and Vey saves his life. He believes the Guard keeps its word. His repaired shield identifies him in the final scene. He is a recurring soldier, not a fourth playable hero.

## The ancient power before the resurrection

The Guard meets **the Keeper** in mission three: a speaking presence held within an old Root shrine, with an Ant-like face formed from carved wood and pale resin. It claims to preserve knowledge from an earlier civilization. It teaches a process that genuinely saves lives and later helps Vey build a relay for the Queen's orders. It speaks to Rusk, Vey and eventually Ilyra, so the player experiences its intelligence well before the finale.

The Keeper repeatedly offers answers to the heroes' particular needs. To Vey it offers practical knowledge that the court has never given her. To Rusk it offers the possibility of leaving nobody behind. To Ilyra it offers a realm that can hear its sovereign without delay. Its recurring belief is that suffering comes from divided wills. Ilyra disputes that claim even while using its help.

The Keeper is the ancient antagonist acting through a limited persona. Its full identity, body and history remain for later campaigns. Its involvement in the Queen's death stays unresolved on screen: do not show an attack, identify the killer or treat the disappearance of the royal signal as proof of a method. The audience can suspect manipulation without receiving the murder's answer.

Vey's royal relay has one living memory core at the field station; its smaller faces convey speech through that core. When Ilyra first uses it, Vey explains that it retains the living pattern that carries her voice. After the death, the Keeper claims that this irreplaceable pattern includes a surviving trace of her mind. Other shrines can still speak with the Keeper, but cannot supply that particular trace if the core is destroyed. The authenticity of its apparent Queen remains uncertain. A living transfer connection is required by the proposed restoration procedure from the moment it is introduced; its risk must not appear as a new rule immediately before the ritual.

Ordinary processed Root remains a useful resource. The exceptional danger is the rite that joins the restored Queen's authority to the ancient source and the relays prepared across her realm. The catastrophe does not retroactively make every normal Root purchase a mistake. How the factions continue processing Root after this event belongs to later campaign design.

## Mission 01 Those who wait

**Playable operation:** Establish a relief base and break Serrik's siege of a frontier outpost. The outpost was built under the Queen's expanding military charter; Serrik considers it a permanent occupation disguised as temporary protection.

Rusk commits to rescuing Vey and the trapped soldiers instead of pursuing a retreating enemy force. He wins the rescue, but Serrik retains the crossing needed for the wider campaign. Brann gives Tarn responsibility for bringing the wounded through the breach. Serrik stops his archers from firing on the wounded, establishing the opponent Rusk knows.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. Vey braces a splintered outpost gate beside Tarn. His acorn helmet is chipped; an ivory leaf patch repairs his wooden shield. The defenders are exhausted rather than anonymous figures in a panorama.
2. Rusk receives two urgent reports at the relief position: the enemy is withdrawing toward the crossing, and the outpost will not hold. He places the crossing marker aside and commits to the rescue.
3. Inside the breached gate, Rusk steadies Tarn's shaking shield. "Stay with me." Behind them, Vey recognizes the commander who has come back for her.
4. Brann puts the wounded column under Tarn's direction. Across the fighting, Serrik holds an archer's bow down as the column passes. Rusk and Serrik recognize one another without a reunion speech.
5. Rusk and Vey clasp forearms beside the rescued soldiers. The distant crossing still bears Serrik's banner. Vey: "You came." Rusk: "You were waiting."

## Mission 02 The Queens peace

**Playable operation:** Capture the contested crossing and defeat the command position coordinating Serrik's blockade. Winning it opens the route into the disputed settlements and their underground passages.

In the hive, Ilyra praises Rusk's rescue but asks what he gave up to achieve it. She knows command will keep asking him to choose. She authorizes the crossing's capture under a common military charter; Brann's offer of mutual defense without compulsory command is rejected. Rusk believes a lasting shared command will prevent future rescues from being necessary.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. In the royal chamber, Rusk lays out the rescued soldiers' names before reporting the victory. Ilyra touches his shoulder: "You count the missing first." Her other hand rests on the map's lost crossing.
2. Ilyra greets a family sheltered in her hive; the parent wears a neighboring settlement's colors. Rusk watches her kneel to the smallest child. Her realm's promise has a visible beneficiary.
3. At the crossing, Brann presents a defensive compact. Serrik will open the road for mutual defense, but refuses permanent Crown garrisons. Rusk holds Ilyra's charter. Serrik: "I gave you passage. You brought a throne."
4. Brann's shield line draws defenders from the crossing's command perch while Rusk captures it. The action answers a specific tactical problem; Vey cuts the alarm connection rather than posing with a glowing weapon.
5. Crown troops receive the captured gate keys. Brann looks toward the settlement's departing officers. Rusk: "When they call for help, we will already be here." Brann: "And when they ask us to leave?"

## Mission 03 Beneath the fallen giant

**Playable operation:** A hero expedition follows the tunnels used by Serrik's forces, rescues trapped workers and opens an approach behind his defenses. The heroes discover Root during this military operation.

The Keeper helps the party escape a collapsed passage and teaches Vey enough to recognize Root's regenerative properties. Rusk sees an answer to the cost of his rescues; Vey sees a discovery that could transform an ordinary soldier's chances. Brann insists on getting the living workers out before testing anything further. All three initially benefit from the same discovery.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. One wide view establishes the heroes beneath a fallen trunk, with trapped workers visible beyond a broken walkway. Bark rises like a cliff; subsequent panels return to their faces and hands.
2. A centipede leg punches through the ledge beside Vey. Brann catches her scarf while Rusk shields a worker. The creature's scale is carried by the fragment of its body in frame.
3. Vey exposes a Root filament beside a small carved Ant face. A crushed moss stem straightens near it. The Keeper's eyes light within the resin: "You found the wounded before you found me."
4. The Keeper opens an old root shutter as Brann leads the workers through. Its help is a concrete rescue, and Vey takes a sample with its guidance.
5. Rusk rests beside an injured worker while Vey examines the sample. Keeper: "There are ways to leave no one behind." Brann, already carrying the worker: "Then teach us on the way out."

## Mission 04 What we can save

**Playable operation:** Secure the first Root deposit, build the processing structure and use the newly available mid-tier force to defeat Serrik's counterattack. Limited Root access opens the tier; the upgraded army can then be sustained by ordinary income.

Vey's preparation saves Tarn, who is badly wounded but demonstrably alive. The Keeper's advice works. At the hive, Ilyra honors Vey and orders the treatment used for enemy wounded too. Vey then asks to share the military process with the neighboring settlements. Ilyra refuses until they accept her command. Rusk supports that restriction, creating the first disappointment between mentor and pupil.

**Comic beats, six panels:**

1. Serrik's counterattack breaks the ordinary shield line at the Root site. Brann drags Tarn clear, with the familiar repaired shield beside him.
2. A healer confirms Tarn's breath while Vey prepares Root at the field processor. The Keeper's small relay face supplies instructions. Her hands shake; she proceeds with Rusk's authorization.
3. Tarn grips Brann's wrist as the treatment takes effect. Vey's expression is relief, not triumph. Brann: "You saved him."
4. The newly strengthened troops turn the battle at the breached defense. Rusk commits the counterattack while Vey directs the force she made possible.
5. In the hive, Ilyra fastens an insignia to Vey's scarf beside beds holding both Crown and enemy wounded. Vey asks to share the process. Ilyra: "Heal any soldier. Arm ours."
6. Vey looks to Rusk for support. He backs the restriction, believing it will shorten the war. Her smile disappears; Brann sets a captured soldier's weapon beside the treatment beds.

## Mission 05 One crown

**Playable operation:** Maintain a steady Root supply for a high-tier living-root siege ram and defeat Serrik's coalition stronghold. The ram's continuous need makes the resource's second role visible.

Serrik offers shared defense and shared Root access in exchange for independent command. Ilyra rejects the terms; Rusk carries out the decisive offensive. He honors the defeated troops' surrender, but imposes Crown garrisons. He accepts Serrik's parole and leaves him administering his settlement under the Crown charter; the Queen's death will give Serrik grounds to reject that settlement. Brann sees valuable potential allies made into subjects. Vey's work has won a war she wanted her invention to make unnecessary.

Vey also completes a Root relay that lets the Queen's voice reach the field command. It is a communications achievement, not mind control; Vey explains that its central living core retains the pattern carrying Ilyra's voice. The Keeper proposes that a realm should speak with one will. Ilyra replies that a crown must still hear other people. Her last exchange with Rusk acknowledges the cost of his victory before the royal signal falls silent.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. Workers replenish the siege ram's Root reservoir while its living frame bears the load. Rusk watches the enemy stronghold; Tarn protects the workers instead of standing in an anonymous army line.
2. Across a parley table, Serrik offers the defense agreement and asks to keep his settlement's command. Rusk places the Crown charter between them. The earlier shared compact remains visibly unsigned.
3. The Root-fed ram has broken the enemy position. Serrik lays down his sword; Rusk accepts it and orders the surrendered soldiers spared. Brann takes their names as Crown troops take their gate keys.
4. In her hive, Ilyra lays her hand against the relay's living resin as Vey described in their briefing. Keeper: "One will. No one abandoned." Ilyra: "A crown still has to listen." Keep her physical location unmistakable.
5. At the field relay, Rusk waits for Ilyra's reply to his victory report. Her light goes dark. Vey reaches for the controls; even the Keeper's small face offers no answer.

## Mission 06 The silent hive

**Playable operation:** With the three heroes and a small escort, cross contested hive districts, reach the royal chamber and recover the Queen. The main army cannot simply march through a capital whose garrisons now obey conflicting commanders.

Ilyra died while the Guard was away. The audience sees no murder, lethal experiment or definitive culprit. Formerly compelled troops have rejected Crown orders; some defend their families, some seize authority, and some still believe the General will save them. The royal attendant confirms death. The Keeper's help with Root has not taught Vey how to undo it.

**Comic beats, six panels:**

1. One wide view shows smoke, deserted routes and competing banners around the familiar hive entrance. The Guard's size is small against home, not an army returning triumphantly.
2. A sentry recognizes Rusk but refuses to open a district gate without their local commander's order. "Her seal is no one's order now." Rusk's reputation can earn conversation, not obedience.
3. Brann shelters civilians beneath his broad shield while Vey cuts through a sealed passage. Tarn leads the people Brann entrusts to him; the contested banners remain visible behind them.
4. A royal attendant waits outside Ilyra's room. Rusk opens the door. Vey and Brann stay at the threshold, already reading the attendant's face.
5. Ilyra lies still on the resting platform seen in earlier hive scenes. No wound or incriminating prop identifies what happened. The attendant confirms the healers found her dead.
6. Rusk holds the hand that once rested on his shoulder. Behind him, Brann turns toward fighting in the hive. Vey lays down the Root sample she cannot yet use to help: "I built the thing that went silent."

## Mission 07 The broken oath

**Playable operation:** Build a foothold within the royal district, break the hostile siege positions and protect the surviving brood chambers. Victory keeps part of the hive alive while the realm continues breaking apart.

Serrik's former troops are freeing settlements from Crown command. He wants negotiated withdrawal and their officers released, rather than recognition of Rusk as the Queen's replacement. Brann begins a common defense with local commanders; Rusk insists Crown authority is the only institution capable of holding the larger realm together. Their military success leaves the question of a future unanswered.

Brann's practical alternative is to return the gate keys, negotiate shared defense and evacuate exposed districts. It saves people but loses territory and much of Ilyra's project. Vey proposes consulting the Keeper, whose continued existence suggests that Root can preserve more than living tissue. Rusk chooses to pursue restoration before accepting the realm's dissolution.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. In a damaged command room, messengers return the Crown's muster seals. Rusk calls for reinforcements that will not come; Brann marks which local companies will still defend the brood chambers together.
2. Tarn, acting on Brann's training, directs a mixed company to hold a passage while civilians cross behind them. Its soldiers wear two settlement colors. Shared defense is shown working under fire.
3. Rusk defeats the hostile siege position overlooking the royal district. The surviving brood chambers remain protected, but three distant Crown banners have come down. The image makes the limited victory clear.
4. Brann places the captured gate keys beside a withdrawal route. "We can keep them alive. We cannot keep all of this." Rusk looks from the keys to Ilyra's empty seat.
5. Vey brings the Keeper's silent relay into the room where attendants prepare Ilyra's leaf cocoon. "It remembers a civilization that is dead. Let me ask what it remembers about bringing someone back." Rusk gives her the expedition order.

## Mission 08 The Keepers prison

**Playable operation:** Capture the fortified relay station held by Serrik's commanders and recover the royal relay's unique living memory core. They intend to dismantle the Crown's communications network, which they regard as the means by which an occupation will be reimposed. Returning to the original shrine could recover the Keeper's general knowledge, but not Ilyra's living pattern held in this core.

Brann is negotiating a handover of the station that preserves local Root treatment while ending Crown command. Vey learns that the relay still responds with Ilyra's voice; its unique core is about to be destroyed. She fears losing both the apparent survivor and the chance to understand whether her invention harmed Ilyra. She launches the attack before those talks conclude. Rusk commits troops to support her, then holds the captured command officers as leverage against further dismantling. This defeats Brann's political work and turns the search for restoration into a new grievance.

The recovered Keeper can produce a responsive image of Ilyra's remembered voice, rather than merely repeat a recording. It claims her mind's trace in the relay core can be restored at an old sanctuary where body and memory are reunited through a living connection. This is persuasive evidence of an intelligence, not proof that it is Ilyra. Vey believes her alchemy can complete what the Keeper preserves. Ilyra's remains leave the hive in the same resin-lined leaf cocoon, on a bark bier with two bearers.

**Comic beats, six panels:**

1. Brann and Serrik's officers meet under a truce banner beside the relay station. Vey sees workers preparing to split the unique memory core; from it, Ilyra's voice calls her name. She decides to act before the agreement is finished.
2. Vey's assault party reaches the station controls as resistance begins. Rusk arrives with reinforcements. He supports his pupil's decision instead of ordering the withdrawal Brann calls for.
3. Captured officers stand beneath the fallen truce banner. Rusk orders them detained until the remaining relays are left intact. Brann: "I gave them my word." Rusk: "I will answer for this."
4. The restored relay speaks in Ilyra's voice. Rusk asks about a private exchange from her chamber, and the voice answers. Vey sees him believe; the Keeper remains visible in the same resin as the apparent Queen.
5. The Keeper shows the ancient sanctuary's location and the living connection required for its restoration rite. Vey sets the recovered core beside Ilyra's cocoon. Brann examines the new detention order, excluded from a decision whose consequences he must handle.
6. The two bearers carry the cocoon through the hive gate. Rusk walks beside it, Vey carries the relay, and Brann goes with them to seek the prisoners' release before the expedition leaves the disputed settlements.

## Mission 09 Two roads

**Playable operation:** Establish a crossing base and defeat Serrik's interception force so the funeral expedition can pass. Serrik's counterattack follows the broken truce and detained officers; it is not an unrelated crisis arriving to remove a hero.

Brann helps win the crossing, then brings concrete terms: release the officers and return command of the settlements held by Brann's willing garrisons. Serrik will then help those settlements hold a common defense while Brann evacuates exposed hive districts. This is a partial settlement, not the restoration of the whole realm. Rusk releases the prisoners, but refuses to dissolve the Crown charter or surrender his remaining royal command before trying to restore Ilyra. Brann relinquishes his command under Rusk and returns the keys of the districts whose troops follow him. He can implement those concessions; Rusk retains the royal hive's keys and his expedition. Brann chooses the settlement that can save people now.

The affection survives. Shared command ends. Brann takes the released officers and willing troops home to conclude the agreement. Vey gives him her safe Root formula for the common defense, accomplishing the sharing Ilyra forbade. She chooses to continue because she believes she can still repair what happened to the Queen. Tarn also stays, believing the commander who saved him will bring their Queen back. The expedition loses Brann's playable hero and defensive contingent.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. Serrik's interception force attacks the crossing as the bier approaches. Brann holds the near bank while Rusk and Vey destroy the position commanding the route.
2. After victory, Brann brings Serrik's terms beside the released command officers. Vey hands Brann her safe formula on a bark sheet. Their weapons remain lowered; this common defense will have troops, territory and the means to strengthen them.
3. Rusk keeps the royal hive's keys as Brann puts his districts' keys beside Serrik's terms and removes his Crown command clasp. Rusk: "When she returns, we can settle this together." Brann: "They need an answer while she is dead."
4. The veterans clasp forearms. Rusk: "You came with me to save her." Brann: "I came to help you save our people." Vey listens; neither man can make the other accept his meaning of loyalty.
5. Brann walks home with the officers and willing troops. Tarn looks after his teacher, then lifts his repaired shield beside Rusk. Vey takes her place by the cocoon. Their separate paths share the same ordinary root.

## Mission 10 The last gate

**Playable operation:** Establish an approach base and capture the Bee order's outer defenses. The Bees defend a containment site; their opposition has a known purpose before the attack.

The Bee commander recognizes the Keeper's relay and explains that the old source can preserve minds, but its restoration rites returned a sovereign bound to another will. They offer to help isolate the dangerous connection and shelter the surviving Ants. They cannot promise to return Ilyra. They have guarded the source for generations and have also refused to share much of its useful knowledge; Vey challenges that withholding rather than simply dismissing the warning.

The Bee record confirms that a living transfer connection is needed for restoration. The Keeper argues that the earlier disaster came from leaving the source's command channel open. Vey proposes to close that channel, use her relay for the necessary transfer and disconnect as soon as Ilyra returns. The Bees warn that the old source can carry another will through the same connection. Vey believes her own controlled apparatus gives them a chance to interrupt it. Rusk accepts the known risk and attacks. He still orders surrendered Bees spared. The choice has evidence, uncertainty and an available alternative, without granting the heroes an implausible guarantee of safety.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. One establishing view shows the expedition beneath a hollow trunk's lower entrance. Bee wax and leaf fortifications interrupt the path. Keep the heroes and cocoon readable; the Bees stand with their wings folded.
2. The Bee commander stops the first volley upon seeing Vey's relay. Their gaze stays on its carved face. "You brought the prisoner to its door."
3. During the parley, the commander shows the old restoration record and offers sanctuary for the living. Vey holds up her successful preparation. "You knew it could save soldiers. Who did you let use it?" The commander's silence has a cost of its own.
4. Vey marks the command channel in the record and the temporary transfer she proposes. The Bee commander points to the same connection: "You cannot choose what comes through." Rusk commits to the assault.
5. The Guard captures the outer fortification. Rusk accepts the Bee commander's surrender and gives his word that the prisoners will live. Tarn lowers his shield to help a wounded Bee stand.

## Mission 11 The price of return

**Playable operation:** With Rusk and Vey, break the sanctuary's ward anchors and seize its restoration controls while the army holds the approach. This is a hero sabotage operation that changes the final battle's conditions.

Vey disables the old command channel and prepares the temporary transfer already proposed at the Bee parley. Her relay carries the Queen's apparent memory and can connect it to the body. She intends to cut the link the instant the transfer completes. The Keeper encourages her confidence in the same achievement it helped her build for the court. The Bees' warning remains unresolved: another will may cross the connection before Vey can act. The risk is understood before this mission, not introduced as a new requirement after the party has committed.

Vey puts the cost plainly: they may be able to retrieve a person, but cannot promise that the returned Queen will command a realm. Rusk promises they will not force that authority on her. He accepts the brief relay connection because he believes it is the remaining way to save her. Vey knowingly participates; she is no longer merely supplying a precaution to someone else's obsession. Neither understands that control can pass through the connection faster than they can interrupt it.

**Comic beats, five panels:**

1. Rusk shields Vey while she breaks a ward anchor under an ancient guardian's attack. Its splintered support exposes the restoration controls, accomplishing the sabotage objective.
2. Vey closes the marked command channel and checks the outer army's position. The separate side exit has a recognizable broken-leaf arch; the Queen's bier waits beyond the restored approach.
3. The Keeper speaks from Vey's relay in Ilyra's voice: "You count the missing first." Rusk reaches toward it. Vey notices that the old resin face and the familiar voice are still inseparable.
4. Vey holds the relay away from the controls. "I can try to bring her back. I cannot promise your kingdom." Rusk: "Bring her back. The rest can wait." Their agreement reveals that his personal loss now outweighs the political argument he made earlier.
5. Vey connects her relay with one hand resting on its release. Rusk opens the approach for the two bearers. Both make the commitment; the drawing must not turn this into a valve whose unknown malfunction absolves them of choosing the risk.

## Mission 12 The Queen returns

**Playable operation:** Use the remaining army to defeat the final Bee defense and hold the restoration platform while Vey completes the rite. The military victory gives the player the anticipated success immediately before the lethal reversal.

Ilyra opens her eyes and repeats her familiar shoulder gesture. Allow genuine relief. Then the Keeper's promised unity becomes enforced obedience: the returned Queen commands the surrendered Bees killed. Tarn stands by Rusk's promise. He is killed as the first wave of controlled soldiers obeys her. The catastrophe is immediate, not a later discovery that an apparently safe resurrection had consequences.

Vey destroys her relay to interrupt the spreading connection and save a small group. It cannot stop the ancient source now embodied in Ilyra. Rusk must leave the Queen within his reach to protect the living people escaping beside him. The first mission's rescuer now depends on his pupil. His story ends this campaign with a painful choice and an obligation to those his decision endangered.

**Comic beats, six panels:**

1. The final defense has fallen. Rusk kneels beside the Queen on the secured platform; Vey completes the preparation with the relay connected. Tarn and the surrendered Bee commander wait at the outer boundary. The side exit remains identifiable.
2. Ilyra's fingers move against Rusk's hand and one eye opens. Give this small moment its own panel, without a surrounding spectacle.
3. Ilyra touches Rusk's shoulder. He closes his eyes in relief; Vey laughs through tears. Tarn lifts his repaired shield in celebration. They have apparently achieved the rescue they believed in.
4. Ilyra points toward the Bee prisoners: "Kill them." Tarn steps between the first advancing Ant soldiers and the commander. "We gave our word." Rusk turns toward the order, still trying to understand.
5. Controlled soldiers strike Tarn down; his identifiable repaired shield falls beside the Bee. Rusk's "Stand down!" fails. Vey tears her relay from its mount as Root lights race through the waiting ranks. Show Tarn's death clearly without gore.
6. Vey pulls Rusk toward the broken-leaf side exit as surviving Ants help Bee prisoners escape. Rusk reaches back toward Ilyra but moves with the living. In the trunk, her face aligns with the Keeper's illuminated root pattern; the possessed army turns. The ancient source continues after Vey's broken relay goes dark.

## What carries into the other campaigns

Brann and Serrik's imperfect common defense has preserved part of the Ant civilization, so the Beetles encounter Ant survivors with competing loyalties rather than a faction erased for dramatic effect. Their treaty is a small beginning for the later coalition, not a substitute for the Beetles' campaign or a completed alliance.

Rusk survives with people he once fought. He must act on their needs while surrendering his claim to decide everyone's future. Vey retains her practical knowledge of Root but loses the achievement that allowed the Keeper to reach the Queen. Her expertise can become indispensable to confronting the enemy, while her responsibility remains personal.

The possessed Queen's army uses the imperial reach established in the first half to spread the catastrophe. The Beetles take its immediate military and human cost. The Bees must confront both the breached containment and the consequences of an order that preserved knowledge by withholding it. Their own campaign needs internal disagreement and achievement, not a later lecture that they were right about everything.

The later coalition provides the final answer to Ilyra's original ambition. People arrive because they choose to defend one another. When she briefly regains agency, she can recognize that they have achieved the protection she wanted without the compulsory command she believed necessary. Her intervention becomes a character decision prepared by her life, rather than a sudden power granted for the boss fight.

## Visual direction for the next comic

Keep the supplied regular Ant design: clean orange-red forms, bent antennae, expressive black eyes, acorn and wooden equipment, simple leaves, ivory chitin and modest bronze details. Rusk, Brann and Vey retain the previous cast's distinct sword, broad-shield and alchemist silhouettes; their working names do not require new bodies or elaborate ornament. Ilyra remains physically distinctive and stays in her hive before her death.

Use expressions, posture, interrupted work and changes in who stands beside whom to carry the personal story. A clean style can show anger, exhaustion, wounded pride, tenderness and terror. Most panels should remain at close or medium distance. Reserve the principal wide views for the underground descent, the changed hive, the sanctuary approach and the final catastrophe.

Every panel selects one visible moment. Tactical context belongs in recognizable gate keys, banners, command positions, the ram's reservoir and the relay; avoid making every scene another map briefing. Short dialogue is provisional. Exact wording, final names and designs should follow the story discussion.

## Decisions still to settle

- The exact royal succession problem and the remaining brood's prospects, including how Brann's alternative preserves a future beyond an immediate evacuation.
- The Keeper's real name, origins, limits and connection to the Queen's hidden death. This draft establishes its dramatic activity without choosing a visible murderer.
- The rules of the exceptional relay and restoration rite, so neither Vey's confidence nor the speed of the takeover depends on arbitrary magic.
- How much of the resurrection's recognition belongs to Ilyra herself, and what she experiences while possessed. Her later recovery of agency must remain possible.
- The final names of the civilizations and characters. Names here identify this proposed take; they do not settle the naming of the game.
