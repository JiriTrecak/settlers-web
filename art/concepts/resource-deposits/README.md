# Forest resource deposits — concept references

The shipped Amber Deposit and Corrupted Root Deposit use 7 × 7 footprints. Their current reference images, editable Blender sources, provider exports and runtime GLBs live in the corresponding `art/assets/asset.models.resources.*` packages. These concept explorations are retained only for future art revisions.

- **Corrupted Root Deposit v2:** a broken, hollow heartwood mass with heavy radiating roots. Pale wood and olive moss frame a strong, magical magenta resin core that visibly flows into the roots. `corrupted-root.png` records the earlier, less magical exploration.
- **Amber Spring:** a central honey-resin eruption with smooth lobes of solidified amber emerging from split soil.
- **Amber Vein:** a broad, crescent-shaped geological seam; exposed resin follows a continuous line through layered earth.
- **Amber Brood:** several giant, rounded amber nodules uncovered in the forest floor. This is the most compact and regular silhouette.
- **Earlier Amber Deposit:** `amber-deposit.png` records the tree-root direction that was superseded by the three ground-born options.

The full amber deposit and its two partially mined variants were generated as separate meshes; the exhausted soil variant is separately authored in Blender. All four are switched by remaining resource amount during play.
