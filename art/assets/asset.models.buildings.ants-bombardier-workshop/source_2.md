# Bombardier Workshop

Updates `building.ants.bombardier-workshop`, preserving its recruitment role. Reference-based Tripo source has 48 editable parts and packed 4K maps; the original provider GLB is retained.

Runtime: 9,562 triangles, one mesh/material, three 2K surface maps. Width 9.2, depth 7.61, height 6.73; gameplay footprint 11×9 and entrance (0,5). Static building; the roof mortars are decorative.

Team ownership covers blue roof leaves, banners and bindings. Timber, ivory, stone, shells and amber remain natural. Verified saved Blender, finite geometry, packed textures, runtime budget, white/green ownership and rear orbit, and simultaneous red/blue game instances beside the main hall and warriors. Hidden surfaces inferred; small leaf veins and lashings simplified.

Rebuild: `prepare_texture.py`, `node experiments/building-studio/launch.mjs build colony-bombardier-workshop-tripo`, then `node --import tsx scripts/assets/publish-tripo-building.ts colony-bombardier-workshop-tripo`.
