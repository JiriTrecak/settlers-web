# Corrupted Root Deposit

Reuses our original painted giant stump, fitted to the existing 5×5 neutral resource footprint, with broad violet root growth. `model.py` is the editable Blender adapter; `source.blend` retains named geometry and packed bark texture. No team colors or animation. The resource's existing gathering capacity, finite yield and gameplay ID are unchanged.

`node --import tsx scripts/assets/publish-root-resource.ts` publishes the canonical asset. Two accessible expansion sites are maintained in `scripts/maps/threewater-resources.ts`. Game lighting and paths from both starts were checked, as was nearby Rootworks placement.
