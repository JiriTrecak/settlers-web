**Woodland Stone Spring — neutral environment prop**

- **What it is:** Low stone ring with a dark water surface.
- **Asset folder:** [woodland-stone-spring](.)
- **Files:** [Blender](woodland-stone-spring.blend), [Game GLB](../../../../assets/library/asset.models.environment.woodland-stone-spring/geometry.glb). The separate `model.glb` is the studio preview; the linked Game GLB is the published runtime export.
- **Live preview:** [Runtime model viewer](http://127.0.0.1:5173/experiments/woodland-review/index.html#woodland-stone-spring).
- **Geometry:** 480 exported triangles; 1 meshes; 3 materials. One LOD.
- **Runtime features:** Static; no animations. No team-color surfaces (neutral scenery).
- **Game integration:** Connected to Threewater Forest and available through the asset catalogue; decorative scenery.
- **Limitations:** Deliberately simple geometry and shared painted materials. No additional interaction beyond the integration described above. Runtime lighting differs from the black-background studio setup.
