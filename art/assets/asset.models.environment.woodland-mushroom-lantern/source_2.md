**Woodland Mushroom Lantern — neutral environment prop**

- **What it is:** A softly glowing mushroom lamp on a branch post.
- **Asset folder:** [woodland-mushroom-lantern](.)
- **Files:** [Blender](woodland-mushroom-lantern.blend), [Game GLB](../../../../assets/library/asset.models.environment.woodland-mushroom-lantern/geometry.glb). The separate `model.glb` is the studio preview; the linked Game GLB is the published runtime export.
- **Live preview:** [Runtime model viewer](http://127.0.0.1:5173/experiments/woodland-review/index.html#woodland-mushroom-lantern).
- **Geometry:** 196 exported triangles; 1 meshes; 4 materials. One LOD.
- **Runtime features:** Static; no animations. No team-color surfaces (neutral scenery).
- **Game integration:** Placed in Threewater; emissive model and budgeted unshadowed point-light emitter.
- **Limitations:** Deliberately simple geometry and shared painted materials. No additional interaction beyond the integration described above. Runtime lighting differs from the black-background studio setup.
