# economy

Match start stamps a tower, a small house, low-goods piles (plank/stone/tools, ≥ 2 tiles apart), 16 jobless bearers, and one L1 inside the HQ in a spiral around **each** HQ. Extra infantry is the Units strip. The garrisoned tower occupies a radius-40 disk; later huts of that player must sit on that land. A constructed T1 stays empty until a spare swordsman walks in — then it stamps. A second player's first tower may still stamp unowned land. Fog snaps to full sight after each kit. Pioneers and geologists are not in the kit — Recruit converts a bearer.

Each tick the matcher pairs the closest idle bearer of player P to the closest offer P may take. Built huts request `requestStacks`; plans request `constructionStacks` (stop at `required`). `building` huts request nothing. Offers are stacks on P's land, or on P's hut offer tiles (no-land test maps). No partitions. `deliver` is a job: pickup at the offer, drop at the request. Bricklayers and occupy recruits are the hut's player only.

When a plan is flat (`flatten: false`, or protected heights already match the frozen mean) and stacks are full it becomes `building`. Mines skip flatten. Otherwise it wants `ceil(protected/15)` diggers from the player's pool. Oldest unfinished plan takes the pool; later queues wait until that plot is flat (idle only — do not yank a kneel). Diggers and bricklayers are separate pools, each default **25%** of civilians (4 of 16 kit bearers). Tools ±1 each; lowering drops the tool from idle extras. No blades / hammers → no new converts. Scaffold takes idle bricklayers from the pool (2 per hut). Miner occupy consumes a pick first. Ghost is red on a slope; click still drops the plan. Too-steep (>127 mark) refuses place. Selecting a hut paints a pip per placeable origin (0 = green, higher = more digging).
