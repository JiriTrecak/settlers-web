# job

A unit *has* a `Job`; it does not implement the verb. `tickJob` walks adjacent / works / completes. Next jobs are more union members, not fields on `Movable`.

`chop` removes the tree. Bearers leave a trunk stack; lumberjacks take the trunk. Lumberjacks walk to the tile SE of the tree and face nw (bearer click-chop still uses any neighbor). The tree stays intact until the last `fallMs` of the chop, then `stateProgress` 1→0 plays the fall clip. Growing saplings are not choppable. `chop` marks the tree tile until the job ends. `cut` walks a stonecutter to `cutStand` (NE of the rock), faces sw, picks for `chopMs`, decrements stone capacity (removes at 0), and takes the stone in hand. `cut` marks the **stand**, not the rock. `pickup` / `drop` move one goods unit (any material). `deliver` is pickup-then-drop for the matcher. `saw` is the mill work clip. `build` walks a bearer onto a bricklayer spot, converts them, and hammers 1s loops (`tryTakeMaterial` at each swing start). `plant` walks a forester to the stand tile, faces nw, kneels 3s, then plants a sapling on `y+1` (must still be the worker's land); the plant tile is marked while walking/kneeling. `occupy` walks a bearer to a finished hut's door and converts them to the worker. `pioneer` walks to the click then kneels 1.2s per unenforced foreign tile (search 6, then 30). `flatten` walks a digger onto a protected cell, kneels 1s, steps height ±1 toward the hut's frozen mean. Pickup and drop share the bend clip (`BEND_TICKS`).
