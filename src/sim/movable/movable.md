# movable

One unit. `type` is the profession (`bearer`, `lumberjack`, `forester`, `stonecutter`, `bricklayer`, `digger`, `pioneer`, `swordsman`). `health` is combat HP. `forcedUntil` is a forced walk dest — soldier brain ignores aggro until that path ends. `material` is stack goods or a sapling (`tree`). `workplaceId` links a worker to a hut (`null` between plots for diggers / bricklayers). `inside` hides them in that hut (no occupancy, no sprite) until `leave`. `become` converts a bearer into a profession (or back). `goTo` sets a BFS path (drops `job`, keeps `material`). `pathTo` is the same without dropping the job; false if no path. `ensurePath` is the request: already `headingToward` dest → keep the queue, else one `pathTo`. Walkable-but-unreachable dest waits 0.5s before that dest is BFS'd again (occupied dest is a cheap miss — retry). `walking` is the in-flight step; `hasPath` is that or a live queue (next hex busy). `headingToward` is “queue already ends there.” `assignJob` is the assignment and claims the resource tile (`markOf`); `idle` / `goTo` / `become` release it. `tickJob` walks/works. Each step occupies the next tile immediately; `moveProgress` lerps the sprite. If that hex is taken, sidestep a free neighbor, wait, or repath to the nearest free stand. BFS ignores occupancy. `view().path` is the remaining queue after the current step dest. Jobless idle civilians flock: repulsion from anyone in hex radius 2, one step at a time. Pioneers and soldiers do not mill, but they step off a hex another unit is already standing on.
