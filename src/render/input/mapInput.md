`MapInput` uses arrow keys to pan and the wheel to zoom. Play opts into RTS input: left drag selects a screen rectangle; left click delegates selection/explicit targeting to Session; right click delegates movement/context orders (or cancels targeting); Shift is passed through for additive selection. Middle drag or Space+left drag pans. WASD does not move the camera. A is handled by the command HUD as attack targeting. Blur clears held keys and the selection rectangle. Editor brush/grab/orbit behavior remains separate from the play selection hooks.

Play mode also pans while the mouse is within 20 CSS pixels of the canvas viewport edges, including edges covered by HUD overlays. Direction uses the same camera-relative axes and speed as arrows; diagonal motion is normalized. Edge scrolling is disabled during manual camera drags, outside the viewport, for touch input, while a dialog is open, and while the document is hidden or unfocused. Window exit and blur clear the pointer; destruction removes the global pointer listeners. Render resolution does not affect edge width.
