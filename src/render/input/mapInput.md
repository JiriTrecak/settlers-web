`MapInput` uses arrow keys to pan and the wheel to zoom. Play opts into RTS input: left drag selects a screen rectangle; left click delegates selection/orders to Session; Shift is passed through for additive selection. Middle/right drag or Space+drag pans. WASD does not move the camera. A is handled by the command HUD as attack targeting. Blur clears held keys and the selection rectangle. Editor brush/grab/orbit behavior remains separate from the play selection hooks.
