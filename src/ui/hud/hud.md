# HUD shell

Menu and Debug share the upper-right navigation row; leaving remains in the match menu. FPS and camera distance appear inside Debug (Ctrl+F3), not over the battlefield. Hero shortcuts start at the upper left. The sky dial is temporarily hidden through its HUD host; the day cycle and indicator functionality remain intact. When shown, the borderless dial at top center moves the sun and moon continuously with the day cycle and blends the sky through dawn and dusk, without numeric time; four resource tiles align with the command-card columns above the abilities. `ui/settlement/settlementHud.ts` adapts the declarative command card, selected entity information, physical stock summary and production queue. It also owns the minimap and clock slots.

Ctrl+Shift+H toggles the HUD in matches and the map editor; Escape restores it. The shortcut is rebindable under Keyboard shortcuts, ignores text inputs and dialogs, and never pauses play. Visibility resets when changing screens. Dialogs remain available while panels, minimap, portrait and debug controls are hidden.
