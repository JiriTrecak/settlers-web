# hud

In-match overlay: compact fps + cursor always on. F3 / ` / the F3 button expands entity counts, jobs, stacks, sim load. The overlay’s **paths** toggle draws remaining walk queues (sticky after F3 closes). Styles in `styles.css`.

Leave: Menu click or Escape (via `MapInput`). Minimap sits to the right of Menu; speed under the minimap.
