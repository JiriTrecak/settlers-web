# Approved dirt and ground-cover reference

User close-up and clarification, 2026-09-08: ground.png.

Warm ochre, dry, irregular earth is the continuous underlying surface, including
beneath vegetation. Green should come predominantly from low moss-like foliage,
not a smooth green terrain base. Earth has fine arid broken texture, small
clods and shallow cracks. Paths are exposed portions of this same ground.
Sparse stone decals and small loose rocks add separate details.

Use this alongside compare.png when authoring terrain materials and ground cover.
