# Approved water close-up

User reference, 2026-09-08: water.png.

Blue-gray moving stream, retaining cool color and visible depth. Broken pale
white/silver streaks describe directional flow and curl around protruding rocks.
Small eddies, darker pools, subtle shore foam; avoid a flat green surface or a
continuous bright white outline. Mossy angular rocks, reeds, fallen timber and
olive lily pads integrate water with the banks. Use together with ground.png
and compare.png; this is the primary water shader visual target.
